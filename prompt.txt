Make a single-file interactive web app
(HTML, CSS and JS) that loads
Three.js from a CDN: a live 3D
container port operations dashboard
called "Tidegrid" for a fictional "Port
Aurelia", built to feel like a strategy
game.
WORLD (the place)
A bright isometric diorama. High-key
lavender-white ground, soft shadows
matte clay-like materials, royal-blue
cranes, colourful containers, yellow
bay lines, small round trees. A pale
blue harbour along the front edge, a
straight concrete quay with four
berths (B1 to B4), a container yard of
9 blocks stacked up to four high and
colour-coded by carrier, a gate with
truck lanes and booths, a rail terminal
with two tracks, a reefer block, a
control tower, light poles, and a
breakwater with a lighthouse

LIFE (the simulation)
A simple scheduler runs the port.
Container ships arrive from an
anchorage, get a free berth, discharge
and load, then leave. Quay cranes
move boxes from ship to straddle
carriers, yard cranes stack them
trucks loop through the gate, a freight
train loads at the rail terminal. Every
vessel has a status (Inbound, Berthed
Discharging, Loading, Departed), a
progress percentage and an ETA. The
clock is live and sped up.
INTERACTION (the game feel)
Orbit, pan and zoom with inertia.
Hovering anything gives it a soft
outline and a small label. Clicking a
vessel, crane, container or truck
selects it: glow ring, location pin, the
camera eases in, the side panel fills.
Speed buttons (1x, 4x, 16x). Click a
free berth to assign the next inbound
ship.

DASHBOARD (the interface)
Floating white glass cards with
rounded corners and soft shadows
Top bar with search, terminal selector
a Live clock and alerts. KPI cards:
berths in use, crane moves per hour,
gate turn time. A panel for the
selected vessel. A bottom vessel
tracker (Arrived, Berthed, Discharging,
Loading, Departed)
EXTRAS (the wow)
Day and night toggle with glowing
crane lights and ship lights, water
ripples and wakes, seagulls, a ripple
ring when a vessel docks, and a
one-button cinematic fly-through
Everything in one HTML file, no build
step, no external assets besides the
Three.js CDN. Keep it at 60 fps.

Swap one word
Keep the five layers and change the
world. Replace "container port" and the
nouns in WORLD and LIFE:
Airport: stands, taxiways, gates,
baggage carts, aircraft pushing back.
Factory: assembly lines, robot arms,
forklifts, a shipping dock.
Launch pad: launch tower, rocket, fuel
trucks, a countdown.
